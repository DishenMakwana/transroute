# Standards — Naming Conventions

## Conventions
- **Components**: PascalCase (e.g. `TransportCard.tsx`, `FilterModal.tsx`).
- **Files & Utilities**: camelCase or kebab-case (e.g. `formatters.ts`, `seed-data.ts`).
- **Types & Interfaces**: PascalCase (e.g. `TransportWithDetails`, `SearchFilters`).
- **Constants**: UPPER_SNAKE_CASE (e.g. `POPULAR_CORRIDORS`, `SORT_OPTIONS`).
- **Database Tables**: snake_case (e.g. `transport_locations`, `transport_pricing`).
